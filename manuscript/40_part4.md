@part Four | The Recipes | Thirty main recipes and twenty-five simple snacks, written for real kitchens, busy weeks and every level of cooking confidence.

:::note Kitchen notes
- 1 tablespoon (tbsp) = 15 ml; 1 teaspoon (tsp) = 5 ml; 1 cup = 240 ml.
- Oven temperatures are for conventional ovens; for convection ovens, reduce by about 25°F (15°C).
- Eggs are large, olive oil is extra virgin, and "milk" means any dairy or unsweetened, fortified plant milk.
- **GF** means gluten-free when you use the ingredients exactly as listed, including the ones marked "gluten-free". Oats, soy sauce, miso, stock, tortillas and spice blends can contain gluten or pick it up in the factory, so if you have celiac disease, buy products labeled certified gluten-free and keep them away from crumbs and shared toasters.
- **V** (vegetarian) and **VG** (vegan) refer to the ingredients as listed. Many traditional cheeses, including Parmesan and some cheddars, mozzarellas and fetas, are made with animal rennet; look for "vegetarian" or "microbial rennet" on the label if that matters to you. Honey is not vegan, so vegan recipes use maple syrup.
- **DF** means dairy-free as listed. Dark chocolate and some breads and spice mixes can contain milk, so check labels if you have a milk allergy.
- **Allergies:** these recipes use common allergens, including nuts, peanuts, sesame, soy, eggs, fish and shellfish. Read each ingredient list, check every packaged product, and use the swaps to replace what you can't eat.
- Protein figures are estimates per serving, rounded to the nearest 5 grams and based on standard nutrition data (USDA FoodData Central). Brands and portions vary.
:::
