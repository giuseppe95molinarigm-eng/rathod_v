@chapter 18 | Weeks 1–12
> Twelve weeks, eighty-four days, and not one evening spent wondering what's for dinner.

Each week has a focus, a 7-day menu, a snack plan, a shopping list and a Sunday prep plan, followed by a short check-in. The protein column shows the estimated total for each day, including the planned snacks; every day lands between about 80 and 110 grams, in line with the 2025–2030 Dietary Guidelines range of roughly 82 to 109 grams for a woman of around 150 pounds. Leftovers are planned in, so most dinners also become a lunch.

Quantities are in the recipes. Before you shop, check your pantry staples: olive oil, salt and pepper, spices, garlic, stock, canned tomatoes, oats and grains. And remember, this is a menu to borrow from, not a test: swap any meal for another with a similar protein figure, and skip whatever doesn't suit you.
