@front A Letter for the Road Ahead
Dear reader,

If you've made it here, whether you read every page or jumped straight to the recipes, thank you for spending this time with me.

I want to remind you of something I said at the very beginning: you are not broken. You are changing. And I hope that, somewhere in these pages, change has started to feel a little less like something happening *to* you and a little more like something you can work *with*.

Think about what you know now that you may not have known when you started. You know why your sleep, your mood and your body temperature have been behaving the way they have. You know that hormones swing before they settle, that your metabolism hasn't broken, and that muscle is one of the best investments you'll ever make. You know there are real, effective treatments, and how to ask for them. You know how to build a plate that carries you through the afternoon. And you know that a twenty-minute practice on a living-room floor counts.

I hope you also know that you don't have to do any of it perfectly. The women I watched struggle through this transition didn't need perfection. They needed information, a little support, and permission to put themselves on their own to-do list. You have all three now.

So keep going, at your own pace. Keep the breakfasts you loved and the strength sessions that made you feel capable. Return to the meal plan whenever life gets busy. Bring this book, or your symptom diary, to your next appointment. Talk to the women around you, because the more of us who say the word "menopause" out loud, the easier this gets for the women who come after us.

This is not the end of anything. It's the beginning of a strong, steady, deeply yours next chapter.

With warmth, and with every good wish for the road ahead,

*Neha Menon*
