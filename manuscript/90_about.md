@front About the Author
**Neha Menon** is the pen name of a writer committed to giving women clear, kind, evidence-based guidance on menopause.

She wrote this book after watching her mother, and later friends she loves, struggle through the transition with too little information and too little support. *The Menopause Diet for Busy Women* brings together the guidelines menopause specialists follow and the research on food, sleep, strength and mood, in plain language and with recipes built for real, busy lives.
