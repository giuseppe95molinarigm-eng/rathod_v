> Thirty days is long enough to build a habit and short enough to start today.

%% Day 1, 6:40 a.m., before anyone else is up. Kalani unrolls the mat her son left behind when he moved out, sets her tea on the windowsill and reads the first day's plan: Mountain Pose, Cat-Cow, Child's Pose, a few slow breaths. Twenty minutes. She's fifty-three and has been meaning to "get back into yoga" for eleven years. Her knees creak in Cat-Cow. Her mind lists the day's emails during Child's Pose. Then, somewhere in the breathing, it goes quiet. She ticks the box next to Day 1 with a pencil, harder than she needs to. One down.

Each day below lists an intention, the poses or strength session, and a breathing practice or meditation script, with the approximate time. The numbers in brackets refer to the poses and moves in Chapter 20. Hold each pose for about 5 slow breaths unless the day says otherwise, and repeat standing poses on both sides.

Strength days are Days 3, 6, 10, 13, 17, 20, 24 and 27. If you need to move a session, keep at least one rest day between the two each week. If you miss a day, simply pick up with the next one.

## Keep Going

Thirty days is a beginning, not an end. To keep the benefits, aim for two strength sessions a week (keep adding a little weight or a few repetitions as they get easier), some yoga or stretching most days, and a few minutes of breathing or meditation whenever you need it. Mix in your favorite days from this plan, or repeat the whole month. And if you'd like more guidance, a local yoga class for beginners or a physical therapist who works with bone health can help you build on what you've started.

:::summary
- Thirty days in four themed weeks plus two integration days, with strength sessions on Days 3, 6, 10, 13, 17, 20, 24 and 27.
- Each day lists an intention, the poses or session, and a breathing practice or meditation script.
- Strength sessions build gradually from 8 to 12 repetitions over four weeks.
- After Day 30, keep two strength sessions a week and your favorite practices for life.
:::
