@front A Letter to You
Dear reader,

If you have picked up this book, there is a good chance something has changed. Maybe you are waking at 3 a.m. for no obvious reason. Maybe your periods have started to behave like they have a mind of their own, or have stopped altogether. Maybe the jeans that fitted perfectly last spring now sit a little tighter around the middle, even though you have not changed a thing. Maybe you have cried in the car, snapped at someone you love, or walked into a room and forgotten why you were there, and wondered quietly whether something is wrong with you.

Let me say this as clearly as I can: **you are not broken. You are changing.**

Menopause is a natural stage of life that every woman who lives long enough will reach. Yet for generations it has been whispered about, joked about or simply ignored. Many women arrive at their forties and fifties with almost no idea what to expect, and with even less practical guidance on what actually helps. If you have felt confused, dismissed or alone, that is not a personal failing. It is a gap in the information we have been given.

This book is here to help fill that gap.

In the pages ahead, you will find a clear, kind explanation of what is happening in your body during perimenopause, menopause and the years beyond. You will learn how food can support your energy, sleep, mood and comfort, and how to care for your bones, heart, muscles and brain for the long term. You will also find realistic tools for the busiest of lives: quick recipes, simple snacks, a 12-week meal plan, grocery lists and a 30-day plan of yoga, strength and calming practices that need nothing more than a little floor space.

Here is what this book will never do:

- **It will never shame you.** There are no "good" or "bad" foods here, no punishing rules and no before-and-after pressure. Your worth has nothing to do with the number on a scale.
- **It will never promise miracles.** Food is powerful, but it is not magic. Some symptoms respond beautifully to changes in what and when you eat. Others need medical support, and that is perfectly okay. You will find honest information about both.
- **It will never ask you to do it all at once.** Small, steady changes made on ordinary days are what make the difference. You can start with a single breakfast.

You deserve to feel strong, comfortable and like yourself again, and you deserve to enjoy the food on your plate while you get there. Wherever you are in this transition, whether you are just noticing the first signs or you are years beyond your final period, this is a wonderful time to start looking after yourself with the same care you have probably given to everyone else.

Make a cup of tea, find a comfortable chair and let's begin.

With warmth,
